Prototype: smoothing the Phone pitch trace

Every lane is a Phone Player singing the same simulated part, delivered to the Stage with real Wi-Fi lateness from the latency runs (about 2–5 holds a second, 50–100 ms, worst about 250 ms). Only the way each lane draws differs. The strip at the top shows, for each reading, how long after it was sung it reached the Stage, lined up with the lanes.

Lane stats over the last 10 s: behind is how far the trace end sits behind the playhead; guess is how much of the line is a flat guess right now; jumps are frames where the end leapt forward; stalls are times it stopped; snaps are guesses that real readings then redrew by more than half a semitone.

Local mics have no Wi-Fi: drawn on arrival, their trace end sits about 30–50 ms behind the playhead and never stutters.
